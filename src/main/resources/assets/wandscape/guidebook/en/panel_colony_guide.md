# Town Panel

> Press 3 with the management panel open; press 3 again or ESC to leave.

The Town Panel is where you pick the town you are working in and see who is in it. The left column holds your town list and any pending invites; the right column holds the current town's members and the players you can invite. You only need it when several players share a world.

## Switching towns

Click a row in the town list to make that town your **current** one: the top bar, the building outlines, the This Town page in Settings and the member list here all follow it. The list is grouped into towns you own and towns you joined, and the current one is marked Current.

Switching only changes which town you are looking at. **It does not teleport you anywhere** — you stay where you stand.

Rows for Ally rank are greyed out and do nothing when clicked: an Ally has no work to do in that town anyway.

## Invites and members

When someone invites you, the invite appears under Pending Invites on the left. Click Accept to join, and that town automatically becomes your current one — again without teleporting you.

The right column lists the current town's members, highest rank first. To add someone, click a player under Invitable Players; the rank they get is the one selected by the Invite Role button on that section's title bar. Who you may invite, and how high you may rank them, depends on your own rank.

The buttons on each member row change that member's rank and remove them from the town. Only the Owner can use them, and nobody can touch the Owner's own row.

## Transferring ownership

An Owner who wants to hand the town over clicks Transfer Owner on the members section title bar: every member row then offers a single Transfer button, and clicking one hands the town to that player immediately. The former Owner drops to Manager.

While transferring, a member row shows that one button and nothing else, so you cannot hit Remove by mistake.
