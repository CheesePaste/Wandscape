# 2. Adventure Track

The adventure track is tailored for combat, spell development, and wilderness exploration.

## Progression

1. **Recruit Wizards**: Hire battle mages at the [Tavern](guidebook:tavern_guide) and train them at the [Mage Hut](guidebook:mage_hut_guide).
2. **Gear & Spell Tactics**: Craft wands at the [Crafting Station](guidebook:crafting_guide) and set spell triggers in [Tactics](guidebook:strategy_guide).
3. **Wilderness Cleansing**: Lead mage followers into hostile camps to claim rare elements.
