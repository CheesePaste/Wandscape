# 2. Adventure Track

The adventure track is about exploring the world. It is playable already, and more of it is still being added.

Adventure Track Goals:

Take your mages along when you head out and open the loot chests in the wild→you gain experience and elements, and the town level rises with them

Spend those on training your mages and making gear and gadgets→a stronger crew, and the reach for more dangerous places
