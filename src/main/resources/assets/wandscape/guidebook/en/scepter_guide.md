# Scepters

> Crafted at the Crafting Station. The four single-mode scepters are available at town level 1; the Omni Scepter needs level 10.

Scepters are how you give orders to your town's mages. You can toggle a mage's peace or follow mode, mark a creature as an ally so your mages leave it alone, or have them focus their fire on a single target. The Omni Scepter folds all of these into one item — shift+right-click to switch which mode you are using.
