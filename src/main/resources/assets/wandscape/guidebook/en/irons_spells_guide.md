# Iron's Spells

> Requires the Iron's Spells mod. Give the mage a spell book in its trinket slot first; only then can Iron's scrolls go into the cast strategy.

Iron's scrolls cannot simply be handed to a mage — it has to be wearing a spell book first. Once it is, the scrolls can go into the cast strategy, and how many fit depends on how many spells that book can hold. Without a book the scrolls cannot be placed at all, and any already placed go inert rather than being lost, coming back the moment a book is equipped. The mage then casts them the Iron's way, handling instant, long-charge and channelled spells alike, spending its own mana, with Iron's cooldowns and sounds, and the Iron's damage it deals still scales with its own spell power. Iron's staves fit the mage's wand slot as well, and when a mage is dismissed the Iron's scrolls it was carrying drop back out intact.
