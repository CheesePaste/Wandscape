# Iron's Spells

> Requires the Iron's Spells mod.

To make a mage use Iron's spells, put the Iron's scrolls into its cast strategy slots — simply wearing a spell book does not make the mage cast what is in the book. The book only decides how many scrolls fit; without one the scrolls cannot be placed at all, and any already placed go inert rather than being lost, coming back once a book is equipped.
