# 0. Getting Started

> This chapter is the written version of the in-game tutorial. Press V to open the management panel, follow the guide box in the top-right through the five steps below, and you'll have a town that works on its own; if you have already done them, skip to the next chapter.

## 1. Build the Town Hall

Press V to open the management panel, then 1 for the Build tab and C to raise the cursor. Click the Town Hall card and double-click it to start placing: hold right-click and drag to turn the camera, put the building where you want it, left-click to rotate it, then confirm with Submit Build and Submit on the right. When construction finishes the naming screen pops up on its own — give your town a name and you're settled in.

## 2. Build a Warehouse

The warehouse is the town's shared stock: everything mages gather or craft goes in here, and whatever sits in your own inventory doesn't count. The moment it is up, every element gets a starting amount in there, and that is what you build with. Building it works just like before: pick the Warehouse card in the Build tab, double-click, place it, submit.

## 3. Put Something in the Warehouse

Right-click the warehouse to open it and switch to the Exchange tab: the warehouse slots are on the left with your inventory underneath, so clicking an item moves it in, and clicking it again takes it back out. This step is about remembering how the warehouse opens and how goods move — every haul later on goes through this tab.

## 4. Build a Workstation

The workstation is where the town does its work: mages take a job and get to it right there, so keep it close to the warehouse. You build it in the Build tab as well: pick the Workstation card, double-click, place it, submit.

## 5. Queue a Crafting Order

Right-click the workstation, switch to the Crafting tab, pick a recipe and click Submit to put the order into the town's task queue. A mage takes it from there: the materials come out of the warehouse and the output goes back into it — never into your inventory, so don't go looking for it in your backpack. To see how far along the job is, press V and then 3 for the task hall. That is the end of the five steps and the guide box won't come back; for what to build and do next, read 《0.5 Recommended Features》.
