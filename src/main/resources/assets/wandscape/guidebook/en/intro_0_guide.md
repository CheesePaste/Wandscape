# 0. Getting Started

This chapter covers the in-game starter tutorial. Players who have completed it can skip this page.

## Step 1: Open the Management Panel

Press the **V key** (default binding) to enter the top-down town management panel.

From here, you can survey your terrain and switch sub-modes to plan constructions.

## Step 2: Build Core Buildings

1. Press **1** to enter building sub-mode.
2. Select the **Town Hall** and place it on the ground.
3. Build a **Warehouse** to store supplies and currency.
4. Build a **Crafting Station** for mages to craft supplies.
