# Permissions

> In single player you can skip this page; it matters when several players share a world.

A town and a player relate many-to-many: you can own several towns and be a member of someone else's at the same time. What you may do is not about who you are, but about **your rank on that town's roster**:

- **Owner** — the town's owner. Only they can change the town's settings, change other players' ranks and hand ownership over. Every town has exactly one Owner.
- **Manager** — every hands-on job in town: building and demolishing, hiring and dismissing mages, scheduling tasks, casting at the altar, paving roads and shaping terrain.
- **Member** — warehouse access and workshop orders, and nothing else: no building, no mage management.
- **Ally** — one thing only: the town's mages will not attack you. No permissions at all, and you cannot even switch to that town.

Anyone not on the roster can do nothing at all, not even spend money in a shop or stay at a hotel.

## Where ranks come from

The player who invites you picks your rank on the spot (Manager and above may invite, and only at a rank below their own).

An Owner only ever comes from a **transfer**: the current Owner hands the town to you from the Town Panel, and the former Owner drops to Manager. That is the only way ownership changes hands — nobody can be set to Owner directly.

## The town you are working in

A rank decides what you may do in a town; which town you are looking at is a separate matter. Click a row in the town list of the Town Panel to make that town your current one, and the top bar, the building outlines and the member list all follow.

Switching neither teleports you nor changes your rank in the town you left.

Which buttons each rank can use in the Town Panel is on 《Town Panel》.
