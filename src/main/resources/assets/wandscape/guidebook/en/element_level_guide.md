# Elements and Town Level

Elements are the town currency. There are seven element types, none of which convert directly to another. Check amounts in the warehouse or the top bar of the overview mode.

## Town Level

The town level is the overall progression: every building in the build panel lists the level it needs, and a mage's rank cap and the Crafting Station blueprints follow it too. You can see the current level in the Town Hall panel.

Raising the town level takes experience, and there are two ways to earn it: satisfying tourists — only a tourist that leaves with all three bars full earns the town any — and opening loot chests out in the wild.
