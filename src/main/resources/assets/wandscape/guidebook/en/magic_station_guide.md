# Magic Workshop

The Magic Workshop synthesizes **magic scrolls** from elements. A wizard only casts what is loaded into the [Cast Strategy](strategy_guide.md) bar — craft a scroll and equip it on a wizard to give it that combat spell.

## Using It

1. Place a **Magic Workshop** from the build panel; wizards run the synthesis jobs in queue order.
2. **Aim your crosshair at it and right-click** to open the panel: the left side lists the craftable spells (searchable by name), the right side shows the task queue.
3. Pick a spell → set the quantity → **Submit**. Finished scrolls go into the colony **warehouse**.
4. Take the scroll from the warehouse into your own inventory, then drag it into the matching category slot on the wizard's [strategy bar](strategy_guide.md).

## Craftable Spell Scrolls

| Scroll | Colony level required | Element cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beam** | Level 1 | Fire ×650 + Earth ×550 |
| **Heal** | Level 1 | Water ×650 + Wood ×550 |
| **Meteor** | Level 1 | Fire ×700 + Metal ×500 |
| **Petrification** | Level 1 | Earth ×700 + Metal ×500 |
| **Enfeeble Field** | Level 10 | Dark ×8000 + Wind ×7000 |
| **Fortification** | Level 10 | Metal ×8000 + Earth ×7000 |
| **Conversion** | Level 20 | Dark ×24000 + Fire ×23000 |
| **Desperation** | Level 20 | Dark ×24000 + Fire ×13000 + Water ×10000 |

## Tips

- Scrolls land in the colony warehouse, just like [wands](crafting_guide.md) — who wears what and in which order is up to you in the [Wizard Panel](npc_guide.md) and [Cast Strategy](strategy_guide.md).
- Teleport, Revive, and similar spells have no scroll recipe: Revive goes through the [Altar](altar_guide.md).
