# Advanced Casting

## The overall strategy

The four strategies at the top — Balanced, Offensive, Support and Defensive — only decide the order between categories. Inside a category the order still follows your slots.

## The casting lock and gear-gated spells

While casting, a mage is held by a casting lock: no second spell starts until it releases, and cooldowns begin only after that.

Iron's Spells and Goety need extra gear: an Iron scroll only fits with a spell book equipped, and a Goety focus only with a Goety wand in the main hand — one per bar. Losing the gear switches those entries off rather than dropping them.
