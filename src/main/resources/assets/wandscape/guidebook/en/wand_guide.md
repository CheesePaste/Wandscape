# Wand

> Crafted at the Crafting Station. Blueprints unlock as your town levels up — 1, 5, 10, 20 and 30 each open a batch.

A wand is a mage's main-hand item, and the attribute bonus it carries only takes effect once it is equipped on a mage, boosting things like spell power, work speed or max mana. The 12 preset wands each lean a different way, some toward combat and some toward building. You can pull a wand off one mage and hand it to another at any time, but a player holding one gains nothing from it.
