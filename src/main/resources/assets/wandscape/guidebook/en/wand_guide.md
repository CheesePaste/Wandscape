# Wand

> Crafted at the Crafting Station. Blueprints unlock as your town levels up — 1, 5, 10, 20 and 30 each open a batch.

A wand is a mage's main-hand item, and the attribute bonus it carries only takes effect once it is equipped on a mage, boosting things like spell power, work speed or max mana. The 12 preset wands each lean a different way, some toward combat and some toward building. You can pull a wand off one mage and hand it to another at any time, but a player holding one gains none of that.

A player holding a wand can also give orders to the town's mages: shift+right-click to switch modes, right-click to use the current one, and the current mode is written in the item tooltip. Shelter and Hostile are the two that work on creatures — one marks the target as an ally so your mages leave it alone, the other sends nearby mages to focus their fire on it. Gather and Identify are the two that work on blocks — Gather calls nearby mages to the spot you point at, and Identify unlocks that block's crafting recipe on the spot.
