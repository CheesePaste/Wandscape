# Wand

> Crafted at the Crafting Station. Blueprints unlock by colony level (1/5/10/20/30).

A mage's main-hand item: equipping one applies its preset attributes to that mage — spell power, work speed, max mana, cast speed, health, armor and move speed. The 12 preset wands each trade one thing for another, and a player holding one gets no bonus at all.
