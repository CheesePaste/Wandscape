# Services

The service buildings with beds are hotels: from evening on, tourists who have not filled their bars look for a hotel with a free room, sleep there and wake with full energy. A town with no hotel, or with every room taken, loses those tourists the same evening.

The rest are daytime stops — farms, fishing points, windmills, the post station: a tourist spends energy and gains values there, and the town takes in elements each visit.
