# Workstation

The Workstation turns warehouse elements into items, and breaks warehouse items back down into elements. Breaking down returns only a fraction of what the item cost to make, so it is for clearing out stock you have no use for, not for profit.

Both directions only touch the town warehouse — nothing in your own inventory is involved. When a shop runs short, the Workstation gets called in automatically to craft the missing goods, so seeing it start a job on its own is normal.

A Workstation works on one job at a time, so anything queued behind it waits its turn. If crafting feels slow, build a second one somewhere else — each keeps its own queue and they can run at once. Mages do the actual work, though, so extra Workstations add nothing while every mage is busy.

## Unlocking Recipes

A new town has none of its crafting recipes unlocked, and the greyed-out half of the list on the Synthesize tab is exactly that. There are three ways to open one: deposit an item into the warehouse and its own recipe unlocks with it; hold a wand and right-click a block in Identify mode to unlock that block's recipe on the spot; or take an [Item Blueprint](blueprint_guide.md) and pick any single recipe on the Recipes page in the warehouse. Unlocks are per town and permanent.
