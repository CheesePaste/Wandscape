# Curios Slots

> Requires the Curios mod.

With it installed, mages can wear trinkets too. Open the "Trinkets" button in the top-left of the mage panel to reach the slots: necklaces, rings, belts and Iron's spell books all go here. This mod's warehouse terminal fits a hands or bracelet slot, where a hotkey opens the warehouse panel — that hotkey has no default bind, so you have to set one in your controls.
