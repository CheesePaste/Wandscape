# Curios Slots

> Requires the Curios mod. Once it is installed, right-click one of your own mages and a "Trinkets" button appears in the top-left of the panel.

With Curios installed, a mage gains a full set of trinket slots, reachable through the "Trinkets" button in the top-left of the mage panel. Necklaces, rings, belts and similar trinkets can all be given to a mage, and Iron's spell books are worn there too. Among this mod's own items, the magic compass fits a charm slot and keeps pointing at the town hall while worn, and the warehouse terminal fits a hands or bracelet slot, where a hotkey opens the warehouse panel — that hotkey has no default bind, so you have to set one in your controls.
