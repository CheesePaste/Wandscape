# Goety

> Requires the Goety mod. Once it is installed, put a Goety wand in a mage's wand slot.

A mage's wand slot accepts Goety's wands and staffs. Holding one is what lets a Goety focus go into the cast strategy, and only one focus fits in the whole bar at a time. The mage then casts it the Goety way: instant foci fire straight away, while charging ones become a sustained volley that stops once the mage runs out of mana, and a volley that gets interrupted costs no cooldown. Enchantments on the focus still apply, and the cost comes out of the mage's own mana rather than your Goety soul energy.
