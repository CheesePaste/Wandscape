# Town Hall

Right-click a Town Hall that belongs to no town yet and give it a name, and the colony exists — only then can the other buildings go up. A player can only have one town.

The town levels up through tourists: only tourists who leave with all three bars full earn it experience, and each level unlocks higher-tier buildings. The Town Hall panel is also where you rename the town, pick the naming style for future tourists and mages, and switch tourist spawning off — with it off, no new tourists arrive and the tavern stops seeing résumés.
