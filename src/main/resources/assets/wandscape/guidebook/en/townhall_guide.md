# Town Hall

Right-click a Town Hall that belongs to no town yet and give it a name: the colony exists, and only then can other buildings go up. A player can only have one town.

The panel is also where you rename the town, pick the naming style for future tourists and mages, and switch tourist spawning off — with it off, no new tourists arrive and the tavern stops seeing résumés. See [Town Level](town_level_guide.md) for leveling.
