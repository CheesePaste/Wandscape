# Town Hall

Right-click a Town Hall that belongs to no town yet and give it a name: the town exists, and only then can other buildings go up. A player can only have one town.

The panel is also where you rename the town. The naming style for future tourists and mages, and whether tourists keep arriving, live in the [Settings Center](panel_settings_guide.md) under the "My Town" tab— those only change your own town. See [Elements and Town Level](element_level_guide.md) for leveling.
