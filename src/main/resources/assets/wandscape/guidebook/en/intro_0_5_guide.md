# 0.5 Recommended Features

The starter tutorial only covers the Town Hall, Warehouse, and Item Workshop. Here are additional essential features.

《Item Workshop》, 《Equipment Workshop》 and 《Magic Workshop》 each do one job at a time, so if they feel slow, build more of them — every building keeps its own queue and they can all work at once. Mages do the actual work, though, so extra buildings add nothing while every mage is busy.

## Beyond the tutorial, these are worth a look

- 《Unlocking Recipes》 Crafting recipes start locked; how to open them
- 《Elements and Town Level》 Understand the economy system
- 《Mages》 Workers and combatants of the town
- 《Altar》 Revive fallen mages, crucial in the early game
- 《Equipment Workshop》 Powerful wands, exploration tools, and boss fight equipment
- 《Tavern》 Recruit more mages
- 《Mage Hut》 Train mage attributes, the core of town combat power
- 《Road Mode》 Level terrain and pave roads
