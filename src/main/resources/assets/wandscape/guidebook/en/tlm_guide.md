# Touhou Little Maid

> Requires the Touhou Little Maid mod. Tame a maid, turn on Home mode, then switch her task to "Town Work".

As long as the maid's owner is the founder of your colony, she can work as a colony worker. Once her task is set to "Town Work" she takes town tasks just like a mage, walks to the work site, and her output goes to the warehouse as usual. In the task management panel she lines up with the mages, her name prefixed with the tag "[小镇工人]". If her hand is empty she is handed a wand automatically, purely so she looks the part, and it is taken back when she leaves the job. She cannot cast spells or train yet, and she does not take guard or altar tasks.
