# Decorations

Decoration buildings serve no tourists themselves; they work on their surroundings. Street lights and signboards radiate their own comfort, magic and wonder onto nearby functional buildings, so a shop or hotel within range does better.

The range is a radius set per decoration building, and the bonus is capped, so placing them far away does nothing. A decoration building's own values do not count toward the town's totals — you cannot pad the numbers with lamps.
