# Shop

Tourists come into shops to buy, and the elements they pay land in the town warehouse. What a shop stocks is fixed by the building; what you control is the maximum stock of each item, and it starts at zero, so a shop you have not set up never stocks anything.

When goods run low the shop asks the warehouse for the real items and has the Item Workshop craft them if the warehouse is empty, so build shops together with a warehouse and a item workshop. A shop with nothing in stock contributes nothing to the town's three values.

Restocking is not free: the goods are crafted by the Item Workshop and the element cost comes off the warehouse books, paid back with profit once a tourist buys. Goods whose crafting recipe is still locked can never be replenished at all and the row is marked Locked. See 《Unlocking Recipes》 for how to open one.
