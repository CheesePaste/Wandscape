# Mage Hut

A Mage Hut does not produce mages; it gives one you already have a home. Pick a name from the town's mage list in the hut panel and assign it — each mage lives in one hut.

Only a resident mage can train its attributes or be promoted, and the panel is also where you look at its gear and strategy or let it rest. Training and promotion both spend elements from the warehouse. A mage's rank cannot go past the town's level, so when promotion is blocked, level up the town first.
