# Item Blueprint

> Not craftable: you get one by chance from a wild chest, or from a tourist who leaves satisfied.

A new town has not a single crafting recipe unlocked, and a blueprint is the key that opens whichever one you choose. Right-click a blueprint, or click Recipes in the warehouse, to open the recipe book; click Unlock on a row to spend a blueprint on it — one blueprint per recipe, unlocked for the whole town and permanent. Blueprints count whether they sit in your inventory or in the warehouse.

Two other routes cost no blueprint: deposit an item into the warehouse and its own recipe unlocks with it, or hold a wand and right-click a block in Identify mode to unlock that block's recipe on the spot. Keep the blueprints for the recipes whose item you cannot get hold of.
