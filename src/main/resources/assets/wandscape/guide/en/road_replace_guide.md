# 🧱 Replace Mode

Used for everyday paving: frame an area of ground and replace the surface grass, dirt, sand, and stone directly with the chosen road material.

## Three Steps to Pave a Stretch of Road

1. Enter the mode: press **V** → the **Roads** icon → select **Replace (REPLACE)** in the mode bar → double-click a card in the bottom bar to pick a material.
2. Frame the area: press **C** to release the cursor, aim at the ground with the crosshair, **right-click** the start point (the ground lights up red), move to the opposite corner and **left-click** the end point (a green selection is pulled out).
3. Submit: check the selection and press **Enter**. Wizard NPCs will execute the paving; once done it becomes a walkable road.

Picked the wrong spot? **Right-click** once to clear and reselect, or **Backspace** to undo the last point. The paving reach is about **64 blocks** — fly closer before selecting if the target is out of range.

## Common Questions

**Clicking does nothing?** Press **C** to switch the cursor back to crosshair mode.

**Picked the wrong start point?** **Backspace** to undo, or **right-click** to clear and reframe.

**Paved in the wrong place?** Switch to **Destroy (DESTROY)** mode and remove it with a reference block.
