# 🧱 Replace Mode

Everyday paving depends on this: pick an area of ground, pull a box, press Enter, done. Grass, dirt, sand, and stone on the surface are paved directly into the road material you chose.

## Three Steps to Pave a Stretch of Road

**1. Enter the mode.** Press **V** → the **Roads** icon → select **Replace (REPLACE)** in the mode bar → double-click a card in the bottom bar to pick a material (stone-brick roads are nice).

**2. Frame the area.** Press **C** to release the cursor, aim at the ground with the crosshair, **right-click** the start point (the ground lights up red), move to the opposite corner and **left-click** the end point (a green selection is pulled out).

**3. Submit.** Check the selection, and press **Enter** when satisfied. Wizard NPCs will come and pave it — when done, it is a road you can walk on.

Picked the wrong spot? **Right-click** once to clear and reselect, or **Backspace** to undo the last point. The paving reach is about **64 blocks** — if the target is too far, fly closer before selecting.

## Common Questions

**Clicking does nothing?** Press **C** to switch the cursor back to crosshair mode.

**Picked the wrong start point?** Backspace to undo, or right-click to clear and reframe.

**Paved in the wrong place?** Switch to **Destroy (DESTROY)** mode and remove it with a reference block.
