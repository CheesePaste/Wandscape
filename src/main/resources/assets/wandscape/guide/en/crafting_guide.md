# 🔨 Crafting Station

The dedicated facility for making **wands**. Wands decide a wizard's combat strength.

## Using It

Place the Crafting Station → **left-click it in overview mode** to open the panel → pick a wand blueprint (searchable by name) → set the quantity → **Submit**.

The task queue panel shows the wizards' crafting progress. One wand takes about **1 minute** of channeling; batch orders process in queue order.

## Wands

| Wand | Colony level required | Cost | Spell power bonus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Wand** | Level 1 | Earth ×16 | +0 |
| **Adept Wand** | Level 3 | Earth ×32 + Metal ×16 | +0.5 |
| **Master Wand** | Level 5 | Metal ×64 + Dark ×32 | +1.0 |

Spell power directly decides a wizard's casting might. Recipes have level gates; when the colony level is insufficient, the corresponding recipe stays locked.

## Tips

- Finished wands go into the **warehouse**; equip them on wizards in the [Wizard Panel](npc_guide.md).
- Wands never lose durability.
- Metal and Dark elements are costly; the Master Wand is a late-game recipe.
