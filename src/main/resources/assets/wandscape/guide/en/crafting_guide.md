# 🔨 Crafting Station

The Crafting Station does exactly one thing: **make wands**. Wands decide a wizard's combat strength, so it deserves its own chapter.

## Using It

Place the Crafting Station and **right-click** to open the panel: pick a wand blueprint (searchable by name) → set the quantity → **Submit**. The task queue panel shows the wizards' crafting progress — one wand takes about **1 minute** of channeling; batch orders queue up, so just wait.

## The Wands at a Glance

| Wand | Colony level required | Cost | Spell power bonus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Wand** | Level 1 | Earth ×16 | +0 |
| **Adept Wand** | Level 3 | Earth ×32 + Metal ×16 | +0.5 |
| **Master Wand** | Level 5 | Metal ×64 + Dark ×32 | +1.0 |

Spell power directly decides a wizard's casting might. Recipes have level gates; when the colony level is insufficient, the recipe stays locked — go level up the town first.

## A Few Tips

- Finished wands go into the **warehouse**; equip them on wizards in the [Wizard Panel](guide:npc_guide).
- Wands never lose durability — craft one and use it forever.
- Metal and Dark are precious elements; the Master Wand is a late-game thing, no rush.
