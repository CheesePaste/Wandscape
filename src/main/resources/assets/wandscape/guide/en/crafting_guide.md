# Crafting Station

The dedicated facility for making **wands**. Wands decide a wizard's combat strength.

## Using It

Place the Crafting Station → **aim your crosshair at it and right-click** to open the panel → pick a wand blueprint (searchable by name) → set the quantity → **Submit**.

The task queue panel shows the wizards' crafting progress. One wand takes about **1 minute** of channeling; batch orders process in queue order.

## Wands

Wand bonuses apply **the moment they're equipped** — Spell Power decides casting might, Work Speed decides building efficiency. Recipes have level gates; when the colony level is insufficient, the corresponding recipe stays locked. New wizards come with a **Basic Wand** (no bonus); the Crafting Station can craft any of the 12 preset wands to replace it:

## Tips

- Finished wands go into the **warehouse**; equip them on wizards in the [Wizard Panel](npc_guide.md). Wands can be swapped freely — one wand serves the whole squad.
- Wands never lose durability.
- Recipes at Level 20+ cost a lot of Metal and Dark elements — late-game savings goals.
