# ⛏️ Element Node

Elements are the town's currency, and nodes are the mint. Wizards automatically come to nodes to gather, sending a steady stream of elements into the warehouse — you just build, they do the rest.

## What Elements Are

The town has **7 elements**: **Earth / Wood / Water / Fire / Metal / Wind / Dark**. Each element has a matching node building (Fire Node, Dark Node...), and **each type of node produces one fixed element** — whatever you want to gather, build that node.

## Using It

Place a node and **right-click** to open the panel:

- The panel shows what this node produces, how much each harvest yields, and how long the channel takes.
- The slider sets the **harvest count** (1~10), and the panel calculates the total output.
- Click **Publish Gather**, and a wizard comes to channel — **the bigger the count, the longer the channel**, but the more you get at once.
- The task queue on the right shows who is gathering, and lets you cancel or reorder.

## A Few Tips

- A node publishes a gather task automatically once built; when the warehouse's elements fall below the daily consumption, the system also automatically **adds high-priority gather tasks** to restock — a lifesaver when elements run short.
- Gathers go into the **warehouse** — check the stock in the [Warehouse Panel](guide:warehouse_guide).
- Short on an element? Build more of the matching node; in a hurry, decompose at the [Workstation](guide:workstation_guide).
