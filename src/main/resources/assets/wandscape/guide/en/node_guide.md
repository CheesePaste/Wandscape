# ⛏️ Element Node

The gathering facility that produces elements. Wizards automatically come to nodes to gather and send elements into the warehouse.

## Elements & Nodes

The town has **7 elements**: **Earth / Wood / Water / Fire / Metal / Wind / Dark**. Each element has a matching node building (Fire Node, Dark Node, etc.), and **each type of node produces one fixed element**.

## Using It

Place a node → **aim your crosshair at it and right-click** to open the panel:

- The panel shows what this node produces, how much each harvest yields, and how long the channel takes.
- The slider sets the **harvest count** (1~10), and the panel calculates the total output.
- Click **Publish Gather**, and a wizard comes to channel. **The bigger the count, the longer the channel**, and the more you get at once.
- The task queue on the right shows who is gathering, and lets you cancel or reorder.

## Tips

- A node publishes a gather task automatically once built; when the warehouse's elements fall below daily consumption, the system automatically **adds high-priority gather tasks** to restock.
- Gathers go into the **warehouse** — check the stock in the [Warehouse Panel](warehouse_guide.md).
- Short on an element? Build more of the matching node; in a hurry, decompose at the [Workstation](workstation_guide.md).
