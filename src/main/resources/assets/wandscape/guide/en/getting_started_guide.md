# 🚀 New Player's Path: From Empty Land to a Tourist Town

This guide gives you a clear growth path. Follow it to build a magic town that attracts tourists, automates production, and keeps earning.

> 💡 Press **H** anytime to reopen this guide, and **V** to open the panel.

---

## 🏛️ Step 1: Place the Town Hall

Press **V** to open the panel → enter **Build Mode** → select **Town Hall** and place it.

- The Town Hall is your town's brain: it manages level, experience, and reputation, and is the key to attracting **wizard NPCs**.
- Without a Town Hall, your town cannot run.

## 📦 Step 2: Build a Warehouse for Supplies

Build a **Warehouse** and store your building materials and resources there.

- The Warehouse is the logistics hub for every production building, and shows your town's **six elements** (Fire/Water/Earth/Air/Order/Chaos) reserves.
- Deposit materials from your inventory so NPCs can use them.

## 🛣️ Step 3: Pave Roads — Tourists Only Walk on Roads

**This is the most important step.** Tourists will only travel along paved roads to visit, sleep, and spend. No roads, no tourists, no economy.

1. Press **V** → click the **Road** icon on the left.
2. Pick **Replace Mode**, choose a road material, and connect your buildings to the main road.
3. Need curves or an overpass? Use the **Spline Editor** to draw elegant curves.

## 🏪 Step 4: Build Shops & Lodging, Start Earning

- **Shop**: Tourists buy goods and pay you coins and elements. Higher comfort means higher prices.
- **Tavern**: Tourists eat and rest here; highly satisfied tourists leave resumes you can recruit as wizards.
- **Hotel**: Lets tourists stay overnight, so they linger and spend more.

## ⚙️ Step 5: Automate Gathering & Production

- **Element Node**: Assign wizards to harvest the six elements automatically.
- **Crafting Station**: Turn base elements and materials into building blocks.
- **Workstation**: Decompose advanced blocks and synthesize high-tier equipment.

Let NPC wizards do the labor while you focus on expanding the town.

## 🧙‍♂️ Step 6: Recruit Wizards, Scale Up

Wizard NPCs are the core workforce of automation — they gather, build, and craft. Recruit them at the Tavern, and set up **Cast Strategy** so both combat and production run themselves.

## 📈 Step 7: Raise Reputation & Level

Tourist satisfaction builds into **reputation** — the higher it is, the rarer and richer the tourists arriving at your gate each day. Leveling up the Town Hall raises your population cap and unlocks advanced buildings.
